# 🎯 SanZone

**The battle royale, played for real.**

SanZone takes the shrinking-zone gameplay of games like PUBG and Free Fire out of the screen and into the real world. You don't control a character. **You are the character.** You walk, hunt and survive in a real open field, using only your phone.

> 🚧 **Status:** In active development

## ✨ What makes it unique

- 🌍 **Real-world zone.** The safe zone is a real place, and it keeps shrinking. You have to physically walk to stay alive.
- 📱 **Your phone is your weapon.** Aim by pointing your phone. No on-screen buttons, no joystick.
- 🎯 **Real-body hit detection.** Hits, including headshots, are based on real players, not virtual hitboxes.
- 🧟 **Solo zombie mode.** No friends around? Zombies come hunting you in the real world.
- 🏃 **Fitness built in.** Every match gets you walking outdoors. The more you play, the more you move.

## 🛠️ Built with

Mobile sensors · GPS · Web & Android · Free and open tools
